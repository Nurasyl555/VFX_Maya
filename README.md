# VFX and 3D Physics (Autodesk Maya)

Portfolio and laboratory projects on 3D computer graphics, visual effects (VFX), and physics simulations created in **Autodesk Maya**, **Bifrost**, **Arnold Renderer**, and **Adobe After Effects**.

## Projects Overview

- **1_snow_globe & 1_2_snow_globe**: Particle physics & fluid dynamics inside a snow globe.
- **2_dissolve_text**: Particle disintegration and text dissolve effects.
- **lab3 - lab9**: Visual effects laboratory exercises (fluids, explosions, rigid body physics, portals, cloth simulations).
- **FINAL & FINAL_2**: Capstone VFX scenes (castle siege, destruction, cloth simulations, fluid dynamics).

## Tech Stack
- **Autodesk Maya** (.mb, .ma, MEL scripting)
- **Bifrost** & **Maya Fluids** (Liquid, smoke, explosion simulations)
- **nDynamics** (nCloth, nParticles, hair/fur)
- **Arnold Renderer**
- **Adobe After Effects** (.aep)
